How the engine reads

It segments your copy into ordered sentences and sections, then reasons about structure and sequence — not lone keywords. Each diagnostic has an eligibility gate, a threshold, counterevidence, and a specific abstention rule. Confirmed findings cite the supplied lines that triggered them.

  1. 01Eligibility gate. Page type, traffic, commitment and required comparison evidence are checked first.
  2. 02Evidence pairing. Confirmed findings cite the rule and the supplied lines that triggered it.
  3. 03Specificity routing. Narrow diagnoses replace duplicate parent labels instead of stacking the same problem.
  4. 04Explicit abstention. Missing evidence returns “insufficient,” not a confident guess or a false clear.

Why gaps cost you buyers

METHOD

A skeptical buyer doesn't leave because your offer is bad. They leave because a page asked them to feel confident before it earned the right to. Every gap is a small moment of hesitation — and hesitation compounds.

The Trust Gap Protocol names those moments so they stop being invisible. You can't fix a leak you can't see. This tool makes them visible.
